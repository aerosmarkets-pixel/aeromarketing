Where the money actually goes missing

None of these are ad problems. All of them are happening right now in most local businesses, and all of them are quiet.

01

The call you missed at 2pm

You were under a sink. They called, got voicemail, hung up without leaving one, and called the next number on the list. You will never know that happened, and it happens most days.

02

The form you found on Thursday

They submitted Monday night. By Thursday they have already had somebody out. Speed matters more than almost anything else and a form inbox is not speed.

03

The quote you sent and forgot

They did not say no. They said they would think about it, and then life happened for both of you. Nobody followed up. That job is still out there unclaimed.

04

The customer who would have reviewed you

They were thrilled. They meant to leave a review. You meant to ask. Neither happened and your Google profile stayed at eleven reviews for another year.

When you don't need this

×

You already have a CRM that works

If you are on Jobber or ServiceTitan and it catches everything, keep it. We will run ads into what you have rather than sell you a replacement.

×

You genuinely answer every call

Some one person operations really do pick up every time. If that is you, the value here drops a lot and I would rather you spend the money on ads.

×

You are not running ads yet

A system for catching leads matters less when there are not many leads. Get traffic first, then build the thing that catches it.

×

Nobody will use it

If it will not get opened, it is shelfware. This works when somebody actually moves cards across the board. Be honest with yourself about that.
