Why search is different

Meta interrupts people. Google answers them. That one difference changes everything about how the campaign gets built and what a lead is worth.

01

The intent is already there

Nobody searches "emergency roof repair" out of curiosity. These leads close at a much higher rate than social, which is why a more expensive click is often still the cheaper customer.

02

You show up at the exact moment

Not when they might be interested. When they are typing it. For urgent services that timing is worth more than any amount of brand awareness.

03

It works without good photos

Search ads are text. If your work is not photogenic or you have no decent images yet, Google gets you running while we sort creative out for Meta later.

04

The volume ceiling is real

Only so many people search for your service in your area each month. Once you own that, Google is maxed. That is when Meta becomes the growth lever rather than the other way around.

What actually gets built

Most of the money in a Google account is saved, not spent. Here is where.

[01]

Keyword research

The terms people actually type when they are ready to hire, separated from the ones they type when they are researching how to do it themselves.

[02]

Negative keyword list

The unglamorous part that saves the most money. Blocking "free", "diy", "salary", "how to", and the hundred other terms that burn budget on people who will never buy.

[03]

Campaign and ad group structure

Tight groups so the ad matches the search. A person searching one service should not land on a generic ad about everything you do.

[04]

Geographic targeting

Set to your real drive time, and set to people located in the area rather than merely interested in it. That second setting is a default that wastes a lot of money.

[05]

Ad copy and extensions

Multiple headlines per group plus call, location, and sitelink extensions so your ad takes up more of the screen than the guy above you.

[06]

Call tracking

Half of local search leads call instead of filling in a form. If calls are not tracked you are blind to most of what is working.

[07]

Conversion tracking

Wired up before launch so Google can optimize toward booked jobs instead of clicks. Without it you are paying for traffic and calling it marketing.

[08]

Search term audits

Every week we read what people actually typed to trigger your ad. That is where new negatives and new opportunities both come from.

Where your money leaks in a Google account

Four things quietly drain most local accounts. All four are fixable and all four get checked weekly.

01

Broad match with no leash

Google will happily show your plumbing ad to someone searching plumbing school. Left alone this is usually the single biggest source of waste.

Fixed with negatives and match types
02

Location set to interested, not located

The default targeting includes people merely showing interest in your area. That means someone three states away planning a trip can click your ad.

03

Sending clicks to your homepage

They searched one specific thing and landed on a page about everything. They leave. You paid for that click either way.

04

No conversion tracking

Without it Google optimizes toward whatever gets clicks, not whatever gets you jobs. It will confidently spend your whole budget on the wrong thing.

If you are already running Google Ads, these four are worth checking tonight whether you ever work with us or not. Most accounts we look at have at least two of them wrong.

When Google is the wrong call

Same honesty as everywhere else on this site.

×

Nobody searches for what you do

Some services have almost no search volume in a given area. If the searches are not there, no amount of budget invents them. Meta is the answer instead.

×

Your competitors are national brands

In some niches the top of the page is owned by companies with budgets you cannot match. We would rather tell you that than take your money to lose slowly.

×

Nobody answers the phone

Search leads call. If your phone rings out to voicemail during work hours, Google Ads will be an expensive way to fill a voicemail box.

×

You want the cheapest clicks possible

Google clicks cost more than Meta clicks and always will. If cost per click is the number you care about you will hate this. Cost per booked job is the number that matters.
