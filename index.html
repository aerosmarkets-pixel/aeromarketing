What actually happens after someone clicks your ad

Most agencies hand you a click and call it a lead. Here is the part that decides whether that click ever becomes a job on your calendar.

01

Someone sees your ad and taps it

They are on their phone, in your service area, and they have a problem right now.

02

They land on a page built for one job

Not your homepage. A page with one offer, one form, and nothing else to click.

03

Their details hit your CRM the second they submit

Name, number, and what they need, sitting in a pipeline instead of a form email you find on Thursday.

04

They get a text back automatically

Before they have closed the tab and started calling your competitor. This runs whether you are on a roof or asleep.

05

Your phone buzzes with their number

You get the alert the moment the lead lands, so you are not checking a dashboard to find out you had one.

06

You call while they are still holding the phone

This is the whole point. Everything above exists to get you into that conversation first.

Most agencies build step one and stop. Steps two through six are where leads quietly die, and it is the part nobody shows you before you sign.
